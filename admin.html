Applications to join the city

Dreamers who have applied for a city post that you choose for (the law, the courts, the Gaol, the Treasury and the head posts). Most other posts take the first dreamer who applies, while there is room, so those dreamers go straight into the city and appear under Accepted into the city below. Hire gives the dreamer the post and their entry pack; Turn down removes the application.

Accepted into the city

Every dreamer who has taken up a city post, newest first: their post, where they work, and their two months’ pay in advance.

Places at events and parades

Dreamers asking for a float, a place marching or a stall at an event you have announced, for their business or just for fun. Yes gives them the place; No turns them down kindly. Either way a letter goes to their mailbox. You design events in the game, in the 🎪 Events book (in the Menu).

Events

Every event you have made: drafts, coming and on now. Start now begins it at once (it keeps its length); End now finishes it; Call off cancels it and writes to everyone with a place.

Make an event

The same as the Events book in the game. A parade goes round a whole ring; a festival or gathering is set on the ring’s high street (to put one on a spot of your choosing, make it in the game, standing there).

Gold exchange

        

          

            The cash system is …
            
          

          
One switch for real money in the whole game: buying gold with money, and selling it back. While it is off, nobody can buy or sell gold for money, and this desk and the penthouse need only a password and the door code. While it is on, this desk and the penthouse door also ask everyone for the code from their authenticator app.

          

        

        
Buying: a dreamer asks for gold in the game and pays with the payment link below. When the money arrives, press Payment received and the gold is theirs at once.

        Selling back: a dreamer sells gold they earned or bought, and the gold is taken from them and held. Send them the money by hand to where they asked, then press Payout sent. Refuse gives the gold back. Starting income can never be sold.

        
Paying out real money for game currency can carry legal duties (age and identity checks, tax forms, money-transfer rules) depending on where you and your players are. Every payout waits for you for that reason.

🏺 Rare artifacts

        
Twenty-four artifacts, one of each in the universe and no more than one in any world, each hidden under something plain (a stone, a stump, a paving slab, a cairn, a snow drift) that only gives itself away when a dreamer stands right beside it and examines it. The first dreamer to find one keeps it in their Backpack; what they do with it is up to them (keep it, give it to another dreamer beside them, or hide it again). Nobody can buy one, and no stand-in can ever hold one. Here you see where each one is and who found it. Go there opens the game standing beside it (you see it floating over its hiding spot; nobody else does); Move it opens the game so you can hide it where you stand (Backpack, then Here); Send back into hiding takes it from whoever found it and hides it where it began.

        

        


        
🌀 The Labyrinth

        
A world that is all one maze, with no outside. Old stone (rearranging every twelve minutes) and living hedge (moving every two) grow all through each other, with no rings or borders, and halls from every age stand in it; far from the middle it becomes the Endless Reaches (Deadly), which go on for ever. Right under the middle, the Deep: 64 rooms from every age whose doors seal and open every ten minutes (Deadly), with long stairs climbing far out into the Reaches. The Heart is at the very bottom, far under the Deep: its one way down is a round stone on the floor of one Deep room, a different room every time the Deep’s doors move. Its one hidden find is the Wayfinder’s Thread, down in the Deep (see Rare artifacts). The first to reach the Heart becomes the Master of the Labyrinth until someone else reaches it: they can turn the whole maze for everyone (once every ten minutes), walk the Master’s Way to the Heart, see the full map, and its beasts leave them alone. You can never hold it, and your fast travel goes only as far as the gate.

🎓 Academy tuition

        
Dreamers pay tuition in gold to study at each magic academy: the Somnucor Academy of the Arcane (on Temple Row, with its Spellwright Wing and Grounds) and the Duskwell Academy in Zhaelvorn, under the Underdark. ⚔️ Danger levels

        
How dangerous each world is between dreamers. 0 Safe: nobody can hurt anyone. 1 Guarded: dreamers fight only when both agree (one challenges, the other accepts); nothing is lost. 2 Risky: any grown-up may attack another out in the open; the beaten one wakes by the way in and loses nothing. 3 Dangerous: the same, and the winner takes a twentieth of the loser’s purse (at most 250 gold). 4 Deadly: anything goes, and the winner takes a tenth (at most 1,000 gold). Everywhere: nobody is hurt indoors or round the way into a world, nobody under 18 or under the teacher / parent lock can be fought, and nobody can hurt you, the Keeper. Ironfray and Pyreline keep their own fighting (setting Ironfray to Safe stops fights there). A change reaches players within five minutes, or when they next open the list.

        

        


        
🎄 The elves’ volunteer work

        
At the North Pole, Clementine, the elves’ volunteer warden, stands at the Helpers’ Hut on the village green (east of the great tree, toward the post box). She gives out seven jobs anyone may do by their own good will: firewood for the workshop, hay for the reindeer, letters for the List, clearing the paths, a cocoa round, mending the toys, and stringing the lights. Nobody is paid. Every job done earns a gift the elves make on the spot, one of a kind (no two are ever alike), up to the number a day you set here. Gifts go to the dreamer’s Backpack, to keep or give to a dreamer beside them; never to a stand-in, never sold.

Account deletions

        
A player who deletes their account is anonymised at once: their name becomes “Former dreamer” and their chat, friends, blocks, vault copy and published dream go. Delete for good then removes the rest and the sign-in itself. If the database will not let the sign-in go, the request stays here with a note saying why.

        

      

    


    

      

        
Two-step sign-in

        
While the cash system is off, this desk and the penthouse door ask only for a password and the door code. While it is on, they also ask for the six-digit code from an authenticator app. The switch is on the Gold exchange tab.


        
Keepers of the desk

        
A keeper sees and does everything here. A watcher sees only the Dreamers page — the accounts and the devices — and nothing else: no messages, no orders, no list, no settings. Neither can read anybody's dreamscape; nothing can.

        
Whoever you are adding must sign in to this desk once first, so there is an account to attach. Then add their email here.

        

        

          
          
          
        

        


        
Somnucor — the city's work

        
Every city worker has an office in the tower, and you assign it. Assigning ties it to them on the books only — the office stays locked until a keeper sets a working code for it, on the Somnucor city tab.

        

          
          
          
          
          
        

        

        


        
Positions and wages

        
How many of each post the city keeps is yours alone. You can set any post’s wage here: type the gold, choose an hour or a week, and press Set wage. A business head can also set their own. Every change shows in the wage log on the Somnucor city tab.

        

        


        
Shifts and the city clock

        
Every job in Somnucor is worked in shifts, in city time — one clock for every dreamer, wherever they are. Workers clock in at their post inside their hours and are paid for the minutes they actually work. Choose the city's time zone (for example America/New_York, Europe/London, America/Phoenix).

Payments (Stripe)

        
SomnuMatrix is sold once, for life. Buyers pay on Stripe's own page, through a Payment Link from your Stripe account; Stripe then tells this project who paid, and they are made a member. Nothing here is tied to anyone else's account. The Picture House feed

        
The Picture House shows public-domain films from the Internet Archive on its five screens. A real cinema can plug in here: give a screen an embed address (the cinema's own player, stream or trailer page, https only) or an mp4 address (a trailer or film as a video file, which plays right on the in-game screen), showtimes such as [{"at":"19:30","book":"https://..."}], and a book address (where people buy seats in the real world). An optional programme_url is read by every dreamer's browser for fresh listings, so it must allow cross-site reading (CORS). Leave a screen out to keep its own programme. docs/notes/cinema.md explains it in full. A chain can also send this itself with a partner key (Partners, below).
