Applications to join the city

Events

Gold exchange

        

          

            The cash system is …
            
          

          
One switch for real money in the whole game: buying gold with money, and selling it back. While it is off, nobody can buy or sell gold for money, and this desk and the penthouse need only a password and the door code. While it is on, this desk and the penthouse door also ask everyone for the code from their authenticator app.

          

        

        
Buying: a dreamer asks for gold in the game and pays with the payment link below. When the money arrives, press Payment received and the gold is theirs at once.

        Selling back: a dreamer sells gold they earned or bought, and the gold is taken from them and held. Send them the money by hand to where they asked, then press Payout sent. Refuse gives the gold back. Starting income can never be sold.

        
Paying out real money for game currency can carry legal duties (age and identity checks, tax forms, money-transfer rules) depending on where you and your players are. Every payout waits for you for that reason.

🏺 Rare artifacts

        
Twenty-four artifacts, one of each in the universe and no more than one in any world, each hidden under something plain (a stone, a stump, a paving slab, a cairn, a snow drift) that only gives itself away when a dreamer stands right beside it and examines it. 🌀 The Labyrinth

        
A world that is all one maze, with no outside. Old stone (rearranging every twelve minutes) and living hedge (moving every two) grow all through each other, with no rings or borders, and halls from every age stand in it; far from the middle it becomes the Endless Reaches (Deadly), which go on for ever. Right under the middle, the Deep: 64 rooms from every age whose doors seal and open every ten minutes (Deadly), with long stairs climbing far out into the Reaches. The Heart is at the very bottom, far under the Deep: its one way down is a round stone on the floor of one Deep room, a different room every time the Deep’s doors move. Its one hidden find is the Wayfinder’s Thread, down in the Deep (see Rare artifacts). The first to reach the Heart becomes the Master of the Labyrinth until someone else reaches it: they can turn the whole maze for everyone (once every ten minutes), walk the Master’s Way to the Heart, see the full map, and its beasts leave them alone. You can never hold it, and your fast travel goes only as far as the gate.

🚛 Living city

        
The everyday work of Somnucor, out on its streets on the city’s clock, the same for every dreamer: bin lorries on their rounds (5:30–10:30 and 14:00–17:00), street sweepers, road works that close a lane on a few streets each day (the crew work 7:30–17:00, the barrier lamps glow at night), building sites that rise a floor a day and move on every ten days, delivery vans carrying the town’s phone orders, the post in the morning and the night watchman after dark. Dreamers can walk up to anyone at work and talk to them. Switch any part off here, or make the streets quieter or busier (phones and headsets always get half as many). People phone dreamers: now and then someone a dreamer has talked with rings them on their phone (an incoming call with Answer and Decline; they speak out loud). Dreamers can turn it off for themselves in Settings > “People I know may phone me”; this switch turns it off for everyone (needs the latest server update).

        Things really happen in the city (needs the latest server update): break-ins, market stalls robbed, fights, stolen carts, pickpockets, paint on walls, someone trying doors, forged notes, boats cut loose, and now and then a major emergency (a fire, a burst water main, the power out, a tram crash, a fall in the mine). Each one is one record, the same for every dreamer: the building it happened at, the person it happened to, the culprit (a name and a look), the damage, and how far along it is. In the street, dreamers see the damage at the building, the tape and cones, the person it happened to, the rider, the culprit running off and hiding, and the repair crew. The damage stays until it is mended. A dreamer with a Dragon Corp job takes the case (Phone > Town > “Happening in the city”, or the button at the building), takes the statement, follows the pointer to the culprit and arrests them; a dreamer who works at the yard whose trade mends it (the Glassworks for windows, the Masons’ Yard for fire and the mine, the Waterworks for floods, the Sign Shop for paint, the Joinery for stalls, the Wheelwright’s for gates, the Engine House for the power, the Transport Depot for crashes) takes the repair and mends it on the spot. If no dreamer takes it, the city’s own riders and crews do, so nothing is left hanging. Anyone can tell the Corp they saw the culprit. Homes are never touched; only Somnucor’s workplaces.

        Work calls and texts come only from these: Dragon Corp dispatch rings riders with reports nobody has taken; the person it happened to rings the owner and the staff of that place, and the Corp texts them how it ended; the yard rings its people with the repair; the hospital texts its staff when people are hurt; and the town’s real phone orders reach the places that supply them. Nothing is made up any more. Calls and texts that come while a dreamer is away are kept as missed calls and unread texts in Phone > Town > “About your work”. Dreamers can turn them off for themselves in Settings > “Calls and texts about my work”. Your emergency calls now come from the real major emergencies.

⚔️ Danger levels

        
How dangerous each world is between dreamers. 0 Safe: nobody can hurt anyone. 1 Guarded: dreamers fight only when both agree (one challenges, the other accepts); nothing is lost. 2 Risky: any grown-up may attack another out in the open; the beaten one wakes by the way in and loses nothing. 3 Dangerous: the same, and the winner takes a twentieth of the loser’s purse (at most 250 gold). 4 Deadly: anything goes, and the winner takes a tenth (at most 1,000 gold). Everywhere: nobody is hurt indoors or round the way into a world, nobody under 18 or under the teacher / parent lock can be fought, and nobody can hurt you, the Keeper. Ironfray and Pyreline keep their own fighting (setting Ironfray to Safe stops fights there). A change reaches players within five minutes, or when they next open the list.
