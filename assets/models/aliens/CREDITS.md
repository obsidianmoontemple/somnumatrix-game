# Alien bodies of the Reach

Each body is an artist's model from Sketchfab, shared under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed for the game: put on the game's skeleton names, given the
game's moves (walk, run, sit, idle, fight), in the `-w` copies given a primitive wrap, beads and wrist wraps made for the game, thinned (`-lite` copies for phones) and its pictures made smaller.
Made with `tools/alien-bodies/retarget.py` and `tools/creature-real/rig/tex.mjs`.
