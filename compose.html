Welcome to your Composer's Studio

This studio was made for the music of SomnuMatrix. Write it note by note, play it in from your keyboard, sing or play into the microphone, shape every instrument and mix it all, then send it straight into a world as its soundtrack.

🎹
WriteNever written music? Press ✨ Start me a song, pick a mood, and the studio writes a first draft. Or write it yourself on the piano roll, with 39 instruments and six drum kits.
🎙️
Record and editSing or play into the microphone, take after take. Cut, trim, fade and move every take. Play notes on the computer keys, the 🎹 keys on the screen, or a MIDI keyboard.
🎚️
MixLoudness, left and right, low, mid and high, the hall's echo and a delay on every track.
🌍
Share itSave WAV, small files, stems and MIDI, make a listening link for friends, or send a piece to play in a world or on the concert stage on L38.
Every sound here is made by the studio itself, so nothing needs a licence and everything you write is yours. Your pieces are kept safely in this browser on this computer; use 💾 Back up to keep a copy or move to another computer. Listening links and sending music in are for members; putting music into a world is for the Keeper and the composers the Keeper names.
