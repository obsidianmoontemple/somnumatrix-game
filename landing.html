What it is

Your dreams, a shared city, and worlds beyond

Your dream journal

Type what you saw and it builds only what you dreamt. Dream of the same house again and it's still standing. Over time your dreams become a world of your own.

Questions

The things people ask

Is AI involved?

Yes. An AI reader goes through every dream you write so it can build exactly what you saw, and it gives the people you dreamt a voice. It doesn't invent things you didn't dream.

What happens to my dreams?

Your journal stays on your device. The words go through the AI reader to be built and aren't kept by us. The shared city only ever sees what you choose to bring into it.

How do I play in VR?

Open the Browser app in your Meta Quest 2, come to this page and press Play in VR. Nothing to install. Nothing in VR flashes, and turning is always smooth.

Is it therapy?

No. SomnuMatrix isn't a medical device and makes no treatment claims. If dreams are troubling you, talk to someone qualified.
