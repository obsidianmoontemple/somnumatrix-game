Cinemas

The Picture House in Somnucor has five screens. Your listings can take over any of them: the trailer plays on the in-game screen, the boards outside show today's showtimes, and every showtime is a button that opens your own booking page. There are also cinema spaces in the city and in the World Mall's Grand Arcade that can carry your name.

What you get

  • Your films and trailers on the in-game screens
  • Showtimes that link to your real ticket pages
  • A named cinema space, if you want one
  • Tickets are sold on your own site. Placement terms are agreed with us before you go live.

How to connect

  1. Ask for your partner key (button below).
  2. Send your listings as one simple message whenever they change.
  3. No time to build it? Email us your listings and we put them up for you.

Banks and payments

Somnucor has bank spaces in the city and in the Grand Arcade. A real bank or money app can hold one under its own name. Separately, the city's gold bank can be linked to your payments account, so a player's gold purchase or payout settles by itself instead of by hand.

What you get

  • A bank space in the city or the Grand Arcade under your name
  • A place in front of families: the city has a full K-12 school, so money lessons sit close by
  • For payment providers: a ready settlement link for gold purchases and payouts
  • Placement terms are agreed with us before you go live

How to connect

  1. Ask for your partner key (button below).
  2. For a bank space only, that is all: we put your name on it.
  3. For settlement, put the request number in each payment reference and tell the link when money arrives or a payout is sent.

{ "ok": true, "payouts": [ { "exchange": 13, "usd": 1.00, "payout_to": "...", "requested_at": "..." } ] }

  • A failed payout gives the gold back to the player.
  • Calls can be signed: X-Somnu-Signature is the HMAC-SHA256 of the body with a shared secret.
  • Nothing turns real money on until we switch it on together.

Stores

The World Mall is where real stores show their goods. You design your own 3D shopfront, put your products on its shelves, and players who want something are sent to your own website to buy it.

What you get

  • Your own unit and shopfront in the World Mall
  • Your products on the shelves, with your prices and links
  • A small script that shows your newest mall products on your own site
  • Unit terms are agreed with us when your store is approved

How to connect

  1. Open the store portal and apply for a unit. It is self-serve.
  2. Once approved, design your front and add products by form, by spreadsheet, or with your store's API key.
  3. Keep it fresh with a new spreadsheet or the API whenever you like.

K-12 schools and learning programmes

The City School in Somnucor runs a standards-aligned, openly licensed curriculum from kindergarten to grade 12: 105 courses with lessons, unit checks, course tests, grades, attendance and a printable transcript. Schools and online programmes can hook into it in two ways.

List your classes

  • Your classes appear at the City School's front office, library and report card.
  • A pupil who picks one is sent to your own sign-up page with a referral code. Families enrol and pay with you.
  • You report back what became of each referral, and pay the agreed share only on paid enrolments.

Teach your own pupils

  • Your teachers start a class and give pupils its code. The class can be locked so pupils only meet their own classmates online.
  • "My class" shows each pupil's progress, scores and attendance.
  • Pupils keep their grades and transcript.

The school is a curriculum, not an accredited school. A school that runs it can seek accreditation for its own programme.

Colleges

Online colleges can list their courses in the game, under the College courses menu and at the City School. A player who picks a course goes to your own course page with a referral code. They apply, enrol and pay with you, and you pay us the agreed share for each student who enrols.

What you get

  • Your courses listed with code, title, credits, price and link
  • A referral code for every student we send, like SM-1E53957620
  • A monthly statement of every referral and what is due

The terms

  • 10% of the fee for each referred student who enrols, unless we agree otherwise
  • A refund cancels the share for that student
  • Pay within 30 days of the statement. Either side can stop with 30 days' notice.
